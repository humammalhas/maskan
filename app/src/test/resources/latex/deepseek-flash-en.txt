Let's solve this step by step.

We know:

- 7 pens cost 3 dollars.
- We want the cost of 84 pens.

---

**Step 1: Determine the number of groups of 7 pens in 84 pens.**

\[
84 \div 7 = 12
\]

So there are 12 groups of 7 pens.

---

**Step 2: Calculate the total cost.**

Each group costs 3 dollars:

\[
12 \times 3 = 36
\]

---

**Final Answer:**

\[
\boxed{36}
\]

So, 84 pens cost **36 dollars**.
